Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

TalkFav is a personal portfolio project: a free messenger for chats, group chats, calls and file sharing. This page explains what information the app keeps, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email, phone number, country, username, bio, links, profile photo and cover photo. If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google.
  • What you send: messages, reactions, polls, statuses, and the photos, videos, voice messages and files you share.
  • Security information: the device and browser you log in from, a shortened IP address, and login times. We use these for active sessions, login alerts and to stop abuse.
  • Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, your browser gives us a notification address for your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or show ads.
  • We don't read your messages except when you report a problem and ask for help, or when a report about abuse needs to be checked.

Services that help run TalkFav

To work, the app relies on a few providers. Each only receives what it needs:

  • Render hosts the app, and Aiven hosts the database.
  • Cloudinary stores the photos, videos and files you share.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" sign-in.
  • Brevo sends emails such as password reset codes and login alerts.
  • Metered relays calls when a direct connection isn't possible. Call audio and video are not recorded.
  • GIPHY provides GIF search, and OpenStreetMap map images appear when you share a location.

How long we keep things

  • Messages stay until you or the other person unsend them, unless you turn on disappearing messages.
  • Statuses disappear after 24 hours.
  • Encrypted database backups are kept for 14 days.
  • Error logs are deleted after 30 days.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile at any time in Settings → Account.
  • Hide your online status and last seen, turn off read receipts, and limit who sees your photo in Settings → Privacy & security.
  • Turn on two-step verification and review the devices logged in to your account.
  • Export a chat as a text file from the chat menu.
  • Delete your account in Settings. This removes your profile, messages, statuses and files. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 14 days.

Children

TalkFav is not meant for children under 13.
