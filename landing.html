Questions

Is TalkFav free?

Yes. There are no ads and no paid features.

Do calls work on mobile data?

Yes. When a direct connection isn't possible, the call is relayed so it still connects.

Who can see my messages?

Only the people in the chat. You can also unsend messages, set them to disappear, and turn off read receipts.

Can I use it on my phone and computer?

Yes. Open TalkFav in any modern browser, or install it on your phone from the browser menu.

I forgot my password. What now?

On the login page, choose "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a code to set a new one.
